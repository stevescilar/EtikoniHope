Who We Are & Why We Serve

Rooted in Christ. Devoted to the Youth of Mbiuni.

Etikoni Hope is an authentic Christian outreach program born inside Jesus Celebration Centre (JCC Etikoni). We believe that no boy or girl should remain trapped in cyclical poverty when God has a distinct future and hope for their lives.

Our Vision

To See a Transformed Community Holistically

We envision families in Etikoni, Kathama, and Mbiuni thriving with dignity, educated children equipped for life, and young men and women walking as faithful servants of God locally, nationally, and internationally.

Our Mission

We Are For The Youth, Bringing A Future and A Hope

Guided by Jeremiah 29:11, our mission is to identify the needy youth—the poorest of the poor—and guide them through education, mentorship, spiritual counseling, and health support to unlock their full God-given potential.

Our Guiding Pillars

Core Values Driven by the Gospel

Everything we do at Etikoni Hope is anchored in five steadfast principles with the ultimate aim of eradicating rural poverty.

❤️

Love

Unconditional compassion extended to every vulnerable child, regardless of religious background or family circumstances.

📖

Truth

Uncompromising commitment to the Word of God, teaching youth honesty, moral conviction, and spiritual clarity.

🛡️

Integrity

Total transparency in stewardship, donor resources, and program management under church council supervision.

🤝

Unity

Working in deep harmony with local elders, teachers, guardians, and volunteers to build long-lasting communal strength.

🌱

Child Centered

Listening directly to children and youth, protecting their safety, upholding their voices, and designing programs around their needs.

Biblical Foundations

Statement of Our Faith

Etikoni Hope is purely Christian faith-based. Our convictions guide how we care for the needy and equip sons and daughters to serve.

1. The Authority of the Scriptures

2 Timothy 3:16–17

We believe that the Bible is the Word of God. It is without error in truth and is profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness, that the body of Christ may be complete, thoroughly equipped for every good work.

2. The Triune God

1 John 5:7

We believe that one true God exists eternally in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit and that these, being one God, are equal in deity, power, and glory.

3. Salvation by Grace Alone

Ephesians 2:8

We believe in Salvation by Grace and through faith in Jesus Christ. This good news fuels our love for our neighbors, inspiring us to bring relief, healing, and opportunity to those suffering in the village.

Deep Local Roots

“We Are in a Village Where We Know Each Other Very Well”

True community transformation happens from within. In Etikoni, Kathama, and Mbiuni, our team lives side by side with the families we serve. We know whose roof leaks when it rains. We know the boy who walked 6 miles without breakfast, and the grandmother carrying four orphaned grandchildren alone.

JCC Etikoni church administration provides the organized framework to support all youth programs. The administrative staff and the Local Church Council work together to ensure that every resource is stewarded effectively, aligning directly with our call to end poverty and give the next generation an unshakeable future.
