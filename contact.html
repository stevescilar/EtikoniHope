Visiting Machakos

Planning a Visit to Mbiuni?

JCC Etikoni is located in Mbiuni Ward, Mwala Subcounty, accessible from Machakos town and Nairobi via Tala/Kangundo road or Machakos junction. Visitors and short-term mission teams are welcomed with open arms by the local congregation.

Please notify our administration ahead of time at +254 721 850 684 so our elders can arrange hospitality and coordinate village visits safely.
