Accountability Commitment

Church Council Oversight & Zero Waste

JCC Etikoni church administration provides the organized framework to support all programs. The administrative staff and the Local Church Council work together to ensure that every department stewards resources effectively, aligning with the local church's mission, and operating in a coordinated manner to achieve the overall goals of the church.

We do not have expensive administrative headquarters in Nairobi or high overhead salaries. Donations flow straight to the village where school supplies are procured, fees are receipted, and children are fed.
