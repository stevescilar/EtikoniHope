A Four-Fold Holistic Approach

We don’t believe in temporary band-aids. We invest in the whole child—spiritually, intellectually, emotionally, and physically—so they grow strong for God’s glory and community transformation.

Mwala Subcounty Reality

Understanding The Daily Realities of Mbiuni Ward

Mbiuni Ward is home to over 30,000 residents, with ~5,000 in Etikoni sublocation. Casual laborers earn just KSh 150 to 400 (~$1.15–$3.08 USD @ 130 KSh/USD) per day. Here is what children and families face:

Factor 01

The Hidden Costs of Schooling

While primary schools have tuition subsidies, costs for uniforms, desks, textbooks, exam levies, and daily food prevent thousands of children from attending regularly, triggering school dropouts.

Factor 02

Extreme Under-Employment

Most parents rely on seasonal casual farm labor, boda-boda riding, or market vending. Unstable earnings cause severe stress, child neglect, and domestic vulnerability in single-room dwellings.

Factor 03

Drought & Food Insecurity

Semi-arid unpredictable rainfall leaves rain-fed farms vulnerable. When rains fail, food stocks deplete rapidly, causing malnutrition and making healthy child development an uphill struggle.

Factor 04

Single-Room Living & Sanitation

Many families live in single-room mud or thatch structures without proper ventilation or permanent pit latrines, creating disease risks and lacking quiet spaces for children to study.

Factor 05

Deep Cultural & Traditional Practices

Harmful traditional beliefs, superstition, and fear often divert scarce family resources away from schooling. We share Christ's freedom and the love of God to set families free.

Factor 06

Open to All Without Religious Barrier

While deeply rooted in Jesus Celebration Centre, Etikoni Hope extends help to vulnerable children of every background: “God has a good plan for all of them, and we create programs to reach them and grow together.”
